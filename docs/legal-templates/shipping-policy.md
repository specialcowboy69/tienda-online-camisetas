### Address corrections and changes

Check your delivery address before placing an order. If you need a correction,
contact orders@funnyteesforall.com promptly with your order
number and the corrected address. We will check whether the change is still
possible and explain any options and properly disclosed costs. A change may
not be possible once production or dispatch has progressed.

We assess incorrect-address cases according to the circumstances and applicable
law. If the error is ours, we cover the necessary cost of putting it right.

### Delays

If your parcel is delayed, contact orders@funnyteesforall.com.
We will investigate and explain the available next steps. Carrier, production
or customs delays do not remove your statutory delivery or refund rights.

For EU consumer orders, unless another delivery period is agreed, delivery must
take place without undue delay and within 30 days after the contract is concluded.
If delivery is late, you may generally give us an additional reasonable period
to deliver. If we still do not deliver, you may end the contract and obtain a
refund. An additional period is not required in circumstances where the law
allows immediate termination, including when an agreed essential delivery date
is missed or we refuse to deliver. Other applicable consumer rights also remain.

### Missing parcels and parcels marked delivered

If a parcel appears lost, or tracking says delivered but you have not received
it, contact orders@funnyteesforall.com. We may ask you to
check the delivery location or a safe place and help us investigate with the
carrier. A delivered tracking scan alone does not resolve every dispute.

For EU consumer orders, we remain responsible for the goods until you or a
person you designate, other than the carrier, physically receives them, subject
to the statutory exception where you commission a carrier that we did not offer.
We will resolve non-delivery according to the facts and applicable law, including
replacement or refund where appropriate. We do not require you to resolve our
carrier claim before exercising your consumer rights.

### Unclaimed or returned-to-sender parcels

If a parcel is unclaimed or returned to sender, contact
orders@funnyteesforall.com. We will establish the reason and
explain the lawful options, including redelivery, withdrawal or refund where
applicable. Refusing or failing to collect a parcel does not by itself clearly
communicate withdrawal; send us an explicit notice if you wish to withdraw.

Any redelivery charge depends on the circumstances, prior information and
applicable law. We will not impose an automatic forfeiture of the order value,
an undisclosed penalty or a blanket refusal of refund. Necessary costs caused
by our error remain ours.
