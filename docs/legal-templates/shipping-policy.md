### Delays

If your parcel is delayed, contact [TO COMPLETE: verified customer support email].
We will investigate and explain the available next steps. Carrier, production
or customs delays do not remove your statutory delivery or refund rights.

For EU consumer orders, unless another delivery period is agreed, delivery must
take place without undue delay and within 30 days after the contract is concluded.
If delivery is late, you may generally give us an additional reasonable period
to deliver. If we still do not deliver, you may end the contract and obtain a
refund. An additional period is not required in circumstances where the law
allows immediate termination, including when an agreed essential delivery date
is missed or we refuse to deliver. Other applicable consumer rights also remain.
