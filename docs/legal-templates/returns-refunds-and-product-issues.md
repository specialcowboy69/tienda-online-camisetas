### EU consumers: changing your mind

If you are an EU consumer buying our standard catalogue products online, you
have the right to withdraw from the contract without giving a reason within
14 days. Our standard catalogue products are not personalised to your individual
specifications; production after ordering alone does not remove that right.

The withdrawal period expires 14 days after the day on which you, or a third
party you designate other than the carrier, receives the goods. For an order
of several goods delivered separately, it runs from receipt of the last good;
for a good delivered in multiple lots or pieces, from receipt of the last lot

### Refunds following withdrawal

We reimburse payments covered by your withdrawal, including the cost of the
least expensive standard delivery we offered. If you chose a more expensive
delivery service, we do not have to refund the extra cost over that standard
service. For withdrawal affecting only part of an order, delivery reimbursement
is assessed according to applicable law and the delivery cost attributable to
the returned goods.

We reimburse without undue delay and no later than 14 days after we are informed
of your withdrawal. Unless we have offered to collect the goods, we may withhold
the refund until we receive them or you provide evidence of sending them back,
whichever happens first.

We use the same payment method you used for the order unless you expressly
agree otherwise, and you will not incur a refund fee. A credit or voucher will
not replace your refund without your express agreement.
